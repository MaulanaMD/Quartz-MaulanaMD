# Starting Out

The key of starting doing something, especially one that you hope to do consistently and meaningfully, is removing as much obstacle and friction as possible. Rather than rawdogging whatever activity you wanna do, taking your time to prepare on what and how you’re going to do is important. Basically, making what you want to do to be as effortless as possible, to a certain extent.
