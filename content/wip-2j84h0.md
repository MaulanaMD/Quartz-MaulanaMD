Ada beberapa hal yang aku notis waktu baca (dan baca ulang) komik *Nia Liston: The Merciless Maiden* (*Kyouran Reijou Nia Liston: Byoujaku Reijou ni Tensei shita Kamigoroshi no Bujin no Karei naru Musouroku*; entri MAL bisa diakses di [sini](https://myanimelist.net/manga/152170/Kyouran_Reijou_Nia_Liston__Byoujaku_Reijou_ni_Tensei_shita_Kamigoroshi_no_Bujin_no_Karei_naru_Musouroku)), terutama bagaimana ceritanya bisa terasa engaging.

1. ***Have a clear story direction***. Bukan berarti semua harus diplotting dari awal (lihat [Writing by the Seat of Your Pants](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/WritingByTheSeatOfYourPants) vs. [The Producer Thinks of Everything](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/TheProducerThinksOfEverything)/[Developer’s Foresight](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/DevelopersForesight)). Dari yang bisa aku tangkap, yang lebih penting adalah bagaimana arah cerita, terutama dalam jangka menengah, bisa dikomunikasikan kepada audiens.
2. ***Skip telling the boring part of the story.*** *[If it’s not essential, leave it out of the narrative.](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/TheLawOfConservationOfDetail) -Anton Chekov*
3. ***Have other thing for the MC to strive for, and blend it with the story direction.*** Tujuan dari 
4. ***Have a clear sense of progression for the story—characters, plot, power level, etc.***
5. ***Bring dynamics into the story aspects.***
6. ***Gradual character introduction; they might be relevant later than sooner, but the reader need to know them first.***
7. ***Show the “common sense” of the world.***
8. ***Multi-plot advancement? only if you have more story threads that need to be advance. But then again, you should have one.***
9. ***Reset tempo when needed; show that the characters can fail and get up from their mistakes, though not so much that it lost its narrative power.***
10. ***Use common recuring things as setup for the big thing.*** e.g., for Nia Liston (at this point of writing), it’s about tournaments being repeated again and again (school, underground, and the grand tournament that will be held soon).
11. ***First thing that’s explained in the story must be important.*** Law of conservation of details meets chekov.
